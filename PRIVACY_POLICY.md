# Privacy Policy — The Needed Utility

**Effective date:** 10 November 2026
**Developer:** Mykola Kikets
**Package name:** com.github.mkikets99.neededutility
**Contact:** mykola.kikets@gmail.com

This privacy policy explains how the mobile application **The Needed
Utility** ("the App") handles information. The App is a collection of
offline tools (calculators, converters, timers, QR utilities and similar).
**The App has no accounts, no servers of its own, and does not collect
personal data.**

---

## 1. Information the App stores

All information the App keeps is stored **locally on your device only**
(Android DataStore / local storage) and never leaves it. This includes:

- Notes, countdown events, trip expenses, fuel log entries and calculator
  history you create
- App settings (theme, home screen layout)
- A local flag indicating whether the "Pro" upgrade has been purchased

This data is **deleted when you uninstall the App** (or when you clear the
App's data in Android settings). The App does not back up, upload, sell,
share, or transmit this data to the developer or to any third party.

## 2. Device permissions

| Permission | Why the App uses it |
|---|---|
| Camera | Scanning QR codes and using the camera torch (Flashlight tool). Scanning happens **entirely on your device**; images are not stored or transmitted. |
| Notifications | Showing timer and countdown reminders (created locally). |
| Vibrate | Feedback when a timer finishes. |
| Receive boot completed | Re-scheduling your countdown reminders after the device restarts. |
| Schedule exact alarm | Ringing countdown reminders at the exact time. |
| Internet | Loading advertisements (see section 4). All tools work without an internet connection. |

The App also reads device **sensors** (accelerometer, rotation sensor) for
the Compass and Bubble Level tools. Sensor data is processed on the device
in real time and is not stored or transmitted.

## 3. Information the App does NOT collect

The App does **not** collect, store, or transmit:

- Your name, email, phone number, or contacts
- Your location
- Identifiers created by the developer to track you
- Analytics or crash reports of any kind
- The content of your notes, calculations, or other entries

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**
(Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads
SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising
ID and general device information, and may use it for ad delivery and
measurement. This processing is governed by Google's policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your Google account settings
(https://adssettings.google.com) or limit ad tracking in Android settings.

If you purchase "Pro" (see section 5), the App shows no advertisements.

## 5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers a one-time, non-subscription "Pro" upgrade that removes
advertisements and usage limits. Payments are processed entirely by
**Google Play Billing**. The App receives only a confirmation that the
purchase was completed — it never sees or stores your payment card details
or other financial information. Handling of payments is governed by
Google Play's privacy policies.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

- Your in-app content (notes, events, logs, settings) is retained only on
  your device until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data, or
  uninstall the App.
- The App keeps no copies of your data anywhere else.

## 7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your
country) and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children. Ads shown by Google AdMob may be influenced by Google's
own policies for child-directed services; if you believe a child has
provided information through Google's ad services, please refer to Google's
policies or contact us.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g., the European Union/EEA, United Kingdom,
California), you may have rights regarding personal data processed by third
parties such as Google (for advertising and payments), including rights of
access, deletion, and objection to personalized advertising. Because the
App itself stores no personal data on our side, requests about ad-related
data should be directed to Google (links in section 4); requests about
in-App content can be fulfilled by deleting that content or the App itself.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted with a
new effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes
acceptance of the updated policy.
