## Full description

Did you ever wish that all the different utilities you need were right
under your palm? Now you have it. Meet The Needed Utility.

One app. 42 tools. Zero internet required.

Stop installing five different apps for five small tasks. The Needed
Utility packs a whole toolbox into one fast, beautiful, offline-first app —
and it keeps growing.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Calculations — unit converter, calculator with history, percentages, base
converter (bin/oct/dec/hex), roman numerals, color converter, speed &
distance, fractions, geometry, prime checker, number-to-words.

Money — tip & bill splitting, discounts, loan & EMI, unit price comparison,
fuel calculator, savings & interest, electricity costs, salary converter,
trip & expense splitting, and a full fuel log with consumption stats.

Health — BMI, age calculator, daily calories (BMR).

Productivity — notes with tags & search, text toolkit (cases, counts,
reversing), GPA calculator.

Time — stopwatch & timer with alerts, multi timer (perfect for the
kitchen!), world clock, date diff, countdowns with notifications, and
sunrise & sunset for any place on Earth.

Device — flashlight, QR scanner & generator (WiFi and contact cards too!),
compass and a bubble level, all powered by your phone's sensors.

Fun & Random — dice (d4–d20), coin flips, random numbers, a password
generator with strength meter, metronome with tap tempo, morse code, and a
team splitter for your next game night.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT

• Truly offline — every tool works without internet. Your data never
  leaves your phone.
• Material You — dynamic colors, dark mode, and a home screen you can
  arrange as cards or a compact list.
• Private by design — no accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Your notes
  and logs stay on your device.
• One-time Pro upgrade — remove ads and unlock unlimited entries forever.
  No subscription, ever.
• Built with care — precise math, tested code, and new tools added over
  time based on what users actually need.

Whether you're a student, a traveler, a home cook, a driver, a developer
or just someone who likes having the right tool at the right moment —
The Needed Utility lives under your palm.

Have an idea for a new utility? We're listening.
